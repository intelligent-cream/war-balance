                     Lebihan Imbangan Perdagangan Meningkat pada April 2026

1. Imbangan perdagangan mencatatkan lebihan BND1,118.6 juta iaitu meningkat sebanyak
   180.8 peratus, berbanding bulan April 2025. Peningkatan ini adalah terutamanya didorong
   oleh peningkatan eksport sebanyak 75.8 peratus. Manakala import juga meningkat sebanyak
   21.6 peratus.

2. Kedua-dua eksport dan import menyumbang kepada prestasi perdagangan yang kukuh,
   dengan jumlah perdagangan meningkat sebanyak 54.3 peratus kepada BND2,993.8 juta.
   Jumlah eksport dicatatkan meningkat kepada BND2,056.2 juta, terutamanya didorong oleh
   Bahan Api Galian sebanyak 77.6 peratus, diikuti oleh Bahan Kimia sebanyak 19.7 peratus.

5. Sumbangan terbesar rakan dagangan import adalah dari Malaysia sebanyak 67.8 peratus, diikuti
   oleh Viet Nam sebanyak 6.0 peratus, dan Republik Rakyat China sebanyak 5.4 peratus. Komoditi
   import utama daripada rakan dagangan Malaysia dan Vietnam adalah Bahan Api Galian dan Bahan
   Kimia, manakala import daripada Republik Rakyat China adalah Barang-Barang Keluaran Kilang.

6. Import mengikut kategori penggunaan akhir digunakan untuk pemprosesan Barangan Perantaraan
   sebanyak 56.9 peratus, diikuti oleh Barangan Modal bagi operasi perniagaan sebanyak 40.0
   peratus, dan Barangan Penggunaan bagi kegunaan isi rumah sebanyak 3.1 peratus.

7. Berbanding bulan Mac 2026, lebihan imbangan perdagangan meningkat sebanyak 87.4 peratus
   didorong oleh peningkatan jumlah eksport (23.9 peratus) dan penurunan jumlah import (11.8
   peratus). Jumlah perdagangan juga mencatatkan peningkatan sebanyak 10.0 peratus pada bulan
   April 2026.

                                Trade Balance Surplus Increased in April 2026

1. The trade balance recorded a surplus of BND1,118.6 million, an increase of 180.8 per cent,
   compared to April 2025. The growth was driven by a 75.8 per cent increase in exports. Meanwhile,
   imports increased by 21.6 per cent.

2. Both exports and imports contributed to strong trade performance, with total trade increasing by
   54.3 per cent to BND2,993.8 million. Export value increased to BND2,056.2 million, primarily driven
   by Mineral Fuels, which accounted for 77.6 per cent of total exports, followed by Chemicals at 19.7
   per cent.

5. The largest import partners were Malaysia, accounting for 67.8 per cent, followed by Vietnam at
   6.0 per cent, and the People’s Republic of China at 5.4 per cent. The main import commodities from
   Malaysia and Vietnam were Mineral Fuels and Chemicals, while imports from the People’s Republic
   of China were Manufactured Goods.

6. The imports by end-use categories were mainly used for the processing of Intermediate Goods
   accounting for 56.9 per cent, followed by Capital Goods for business operations at 40.0 per cent,
   and Consumption Goods for household use at 3.1 per cent.

7. Compared to March 2026, the trade surplus increased by 87.4 per cent, driven by an increase in
   exports (23.9 per cent) and decline in imports (11.8 per cent). Total trade also recorded a 10.0 per
   cent increase in April 2026.

8. The International Merchandise Trade Statistics report for April 2026 can be accessed through the

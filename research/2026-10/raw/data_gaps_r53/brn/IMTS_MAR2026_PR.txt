LEBIHAN IMBANGAN PERDAGANGAN MENINGKAT SEBANYAK 21.4 PERATUS BAGI MAC 2026

1. Imbangan perdagangan mencatatkan lebihan sebanyak BND597.0 juta iaitu meningkat
   sebanyak 21.4 peratus, berbanding bulan Mac 2025. Peningkatan ini adalah didorong oleh
   prestasi perdagangan yang kukuh dengan jumlah perdagangan meningkat sebanyak 35.4
   peratus, kepada BND2,722.6 juta. Peningkatan ini disumbangkan oleh peningkatan bagi
   kedua-dua eksport dan import masing-masing sebanyak 32.7 peratus dan 40.0 peratus.

2. Jumlah eksport sebanyak BND1,659.8 juta adalah terutamanya disumbangkan oleh Bahan
   Api Galian sebanyak 78.6 peratus, diikuti oleh Bahan Kimia sebanyak 19.8 peratus.

5. Sumbangan terbesar rakan dagangan import adalah dari Singapura sebanyak 52.4 peratus,
   diikuti oleh Australia sebanyak 14.3 peratus, dan Malaysia sebanyak 11.2 peratus. Komoditi

6. Import mengikut kategori penggunaan akhir digunakan untuk pemprosesan Barangan
   Perantaraan sebanyak 57.5 peratus, diikuti oleh Barangan Modal bagi operasi perniagaan
   sebanyak 40.0 peratus, dan Barangan Penggunaan bagi kegunaan isi rumah sebanyak 2.5
   peratus.

   bulan Mac 2026, didorong oleh peningkatan eksport sebanyak 70.1 peratus dan import

              TRADE BALANCE SURPLUS INCREASED BY 21.4 PER CENT IN MARCH 2026

1. The trade balance recorded a surplus of BND597.0 million, representing an increase of 21.4 per
   cent, compared to March 2025. This was supported by strong trade performance with total
   trade growing by 35.4 per cent to BND2,722.6 million. The increase was driven by higher
   exports and imports by 32.7 per cent and 40.0 per cent, respectively.

2. Total exports amounted to BND1,659.8 million was mainly contributed by Mineral Fuels
   accounting for 78.6 per cent, followed by Chemicals at 19.8 per cent.

3. The main export markets were Australia accounting for 31.2 per cent, followed by Singapore

5. The largest import partners were Singapore, accounting for 52.4 per cent, followed by
   Australia at 14.3 per cent, and Malaysia at 11.2 per cent. The main import commodities from
   these trading partners were Mineral Fuels.

6. The imports for end use categories were mainly used as Intermediate Goods processing
   accounting for 57.5 per cent, followed by Capital Goods for business operations at 40.0 per
   cent, and Consumption Goods for household use at 2.5 per cent.

7. Compared to February 2026, the total trade value increased significantly by 88.6 per cent in
   March 2026, driven by an increase in exports by 70.1 per cent and imports by 127.2 per cent.

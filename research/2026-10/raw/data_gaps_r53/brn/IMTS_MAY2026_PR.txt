                         Lebihan Imbangan Perdagangan Meningkat pada Mei 2026

1. Imbangan perdagangan mencatatkan lebihan BND457.2 juta iaitu meningkat sebanyak 79.6
   peratus, berbanding bulan Mei 2025. Peningkatan ini didorong terutamanya oleh peningkatan
   eksport dan import masing-masing sebanyak 52.8 peratus dan 44.0 peratus.

2. Kedua-dua eksport dan import menyumbang kepada prestasi perdagangan yang kukuh, dengan
   jumlah perdagangan meningkat sebanyak 49.0 peratus kepada BND2,690.8 juta. Jumlah eksport
   sebanyak BND1,574.0 juta, terutamanya disumbangkan oleh Bahan Api Galian; Bahan Kimia; dan
   Bahan-Bahan Mentah Yang Tidak Boleh Dimakan.

5. Sumbangan terbesar rakan dagangan import adalah dari Malaysia sebanyak 69.5 peratus, diikuti
   oleh Australia sebanyak 10.5 peratus, dan Viet Nam sebanyak 4.9 peratus. Komoditi import utama

6. Import mengikut kategori penggunaan akhir digunakan untuk pemprosesan Barangan Perantaraan

   peratus, dan Barangan Penggunaan bagi kegunaan isi rumah sebanyak 2.2 peratus.

   mencatatkan penurunan sebanyak 10.1 peratus.

                                   Trade Balance Surplus Increased in May 2026

1. The trade balance recorded a surplus of BND457.2 million, an increase of 79.6 per cent, compared
   to May 2025. The increasse was mainly driven by increases in exports and imports by 52.8 per cent
   and 44.0 per cent respectively.

2. Both exports and imports contributed to strong trade performance, with total trade increasing by
   49.0 per cent to BND2,690.8 million. Total exports amounted to BND1,574.0 million, primarily
   contributed by Mineral Fuels; Chemicals; and Crude Materials, Inedible.

5. The largest import partners were Malaysia, accounting for 69.5 per cent, followed by Australia at
   10.5 per cent, and Vietnam at 4.9 per cent. The main import commodities from these countries were
   Mineral Fuels.

6. The imports by end-use categories were mainly used for the processing of Intermediate Goods
   accounting for 57.3 per cent, followed by Capital Goods for business operations at 40.5 per cent,
   and Consumption Goods for household use at 2.2 per cent.

7. Compared to April 2026, the trade surplus decreased by 59.1 per cent, driven by a 23.5 per cent
   decrease in exports and a 19.1 per cent increase in imports. Consequently, total trade also recorded
   a 10.1 per cent decline.

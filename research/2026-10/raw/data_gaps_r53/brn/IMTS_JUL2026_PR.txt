             IMBANGAN PERDAGANGAN KEKAL DALAM LEBIHAN BAGI BULAN JULAI 2026

1. Jumlah perdagangan mencatatkan peningkatan sebanyak 46.2 peratus bagi bulan Julai 2026,
   kepada BND2,772.5 juta, berbanding bulan Julai 2025. Peningkatan ini disumbangkan oleh
   peningkatan bagi kedua-dua eksport dan import masing-masing sebanyak 30.5 peratus dan 75.6
   peratus. Manakala, imbangan perdagangan kekal dalam lebihan sebanyak BND454.1 juta.

2. Jumlah eksport sebanyak BND1,613.3 juta, terutamanya disumbangkan oleh Bahan Api Galian;
   Bahan Kimia; dan Bahan-Bahan Mentah Yang Tidak Boleh Dimakan.

5. Sumbangan terbesar rakan dagangan import adalah dari Malaysia sebanyak 70.0 peratus, diikuti
   oleh Republik Rakyat China sebanyak 6.6 peratus, dan Nigeria sebanyak 5.2 peratus. Komoditi

                                           JULY 2026


                           TRADE BALANCE REMAINED IN SURPLUS FOR JULY 2026

1. Total trade recorded an increase of 46.2 per cent in July 2026, to BND2,772.5 million, compared to
   July 2025. The growth was mainly contributed by increases in both exports and imports by 30.5 per
   cent and 75.6 per cent, respectively. Meanwhile, the trade balance remained in surplus amounted
   at BND454.1 million.

2. Total exports amounted to BND1,613.3 million, primarily contributed by Mineral Fuels; Chemicals;
   and Crude Materials, Inedible.

5. The largest imports partners were Malaysia, accounting for 70.0 per cent, followed by the People’s
   Republic of China at 6.6 per cent, and Nigeria at 5.2 per cent. The main imports commodities from
   these countries were Mineral Fuels, and Machinery and Transport Equipment.

6. The imports by end-use categories were mainly used for the processing of Intermediate Goods
   accounting for 57.4 per cent, followed by Capital Goods for business operations at 39.9 per cent,
   and Consumption Goods for household use at 2.7 per cent.

7. Compared to June 2026, total trade recorded an increase of 9.3 per cent, mainly driven by increases
   in exports by 2.1 per cent, and imports by 21.1 per cent. Meanwhile, trade balance decreased by
   27.1 per cent.

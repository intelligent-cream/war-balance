 IMBANGAN PERDAGANGAN MENCATATKAN LEBIHAN BND467.9 JUTA BAGI JANUARI 2026

1. Jumlah perdagangan pada bulan Januari 2026 adalah sebanyak BND1,824.1 juta, yang terdiri
   daripada eksport bernilai BND1,146.0 juta dan import berjumlah BND678.1 juta, seterusnya
   mencatatkan imbangan perdagangan lebihan sebanyak BND467.9 juta.

2. Penyumbang utama kepada nilai eksport adalah Bahan Api Galian sebanyak 75.5 peratus,
   diikuti oleh Bahan Kimia sebanyak 21.8 peratus, dan Bahan-Bahan Mentah Tidak Boleh
   Dimakan sebanyak 0.4 peratus.

5. Sumbangan terbesar rakan dagangan import adalah dari Singapura sebanyak 33.4 peratus,
   diikuti oleh Emiriah Arab Bersatu sebanyak 16.3 peratus, dan Malaysia sebanyak 13.9 peratus.

        TRADE BALANCE RECORDED A SURPLUS OF BND467.9 MILLION IN JANUARY 2026

1. The total trade in January 2026 amounted to BND1,824.1 million, comprising exports valued
   at BND1,146.0 million and imports valued at BND678.1 million, which recorded in a trade
   balance surplus of BND467.9 million.

2. The major contributors to the exports value were Mineral Fuels accounting for 75.5 per cent,
   followed by Chemicals at 21.8 per cent, and Crude Materials, Inedible at 0.4 per cent.

5. The largest import partners were Singapore, accounting for 33.4 per cent, followed by United
   Arab Emirates at 16.3 per cent, and Malaysia at 13.9 per cent. The main import commodities
   from these trading partners were Mineral Fuels and Food.

6. The imports for end use categories were mainly used as Intermediate Goods processing
   accounting for 59.7 per cent, followed by Capital Goods for business operations at 35.8 per
   cent, and Consumption Goods for household use at 4.5 per cent.

7. Compared to January 2025, the total trade value declined by 6.1 per cent in January 2026,
   driven by decreases in both exports and imports by 6.8 per cent and 4.8 per cent, respectively.

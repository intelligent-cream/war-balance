                          Lebihan Imbangan Perdagangan Meningkat pada Jun 2026

1. Imbangan perdagangan mencatatkan lebihan BND622.6 juta iaitu meningkat sebanyak 112.8
   peratus, berbanding bulan Jun 2025. Peningkatan ini didorong terutamanya oleh peningkatan
   eksport sebanyak 66.7 peratus. Jumlah import juga mencatatkan peningkatan sebanyak 46.2
   peratus.

2. Kedua-dua eksport dan import menyumbang kepada prestasi perdagangan yang kukuh, dengan
   jumlah perdagangan meningkat sebanyak 58.3 peratus kepada BND2,537.4 juta. Jumlah eksport
   sebanyak BND1,580.0 juta, terutamanya disumbangkan oleh Bahan Api Galian; Bahan Kimia; dan
   Bahan-Bahan Mentah Yang Tidak Boleh Dimakan.

5. Sumbangan terbesar rakan dagangan import adalah dari Malaysia sebanyak 54.3 peratus, diikuti
   oleh Australia sebanyak 11.0 peratus, dan Republik Rakyat China sebanyak 6.6 peratus. Komoditi
   import utama daripada rakan dagangan adalah Bahan Api Galian dan Jentera dan Kelengkapan
   Pengangkutan, terutamanya helikopter.

6. Import mengikut kategori penggunaan akhir digunakan untuk pemprosesan Barangan Perantaraan
   sebanyak 57.1 peratus, diikuti oleh Barangan Modal bagi operasi perniagaan sebanyak 39.2
   peratus, dan Barangan Penggunaan bagi kegunaan isi rumah sebanyak 3.7 peratus.

7. Berbanding bulan Mei 2026, lebihan imbangan perdagangan meningkat sebanyak 36.2 peratus,
   terutamanya didorong oleh peningkatan jumlah eksport sebanyak 0.4 peratus, manakala, jumlah
   import mencatatkan penurunan sebanyak 14.3 peratus. Perkembangan ini turut menyebabkan
   penurunan jumlah perdagangan sebanyak 5.7 peratus.

                                  Trade Balance Surplus Increased in June 2026

1. The trade balance recorded a surplus of BND622.6 million, an increase of 112.8 per cent, compared
   to June 2025. The increase was mainly driven by increases in exports by 66.7 per cent. The value of
   imports also increased by 46.2 per cent.

2. Both exports and imports contributed to strong trade performance, with total trade increasing by
   58.3 per cent to BND2,537.4 million. Total exports amounted to BND1,580.0 million, primarily
   contributed by Mineral Fuels; Chemicals; and Crude Materials, Inedible.

5. The largest import partners were Malaysia, accounting for 54.3 per cent, followed by Australia at
   11.0 per cent, and the People’ Republic of China at 6.6 per cent. The main import commodities from
   these countries were Mineral Fuels, and Machinery and Transport Equipment, specifically
   helicopter.

6. The imports by end-use categories were mainly used for the processing of Intermediate Goods
   accounting for 57.1 per cent, followed by Capital Goods for business operations at 39.2 per cent,
   and Consumption Goods for household use at 3.7 per cent.

7. Compared to May 2026, the trade surplus increased by 36.2 per cent, driven by a 0.4 per cent
   increase in exports, while total imports recorded a decrease by 14.3 per cent. Consequently, total
   trade also recorded a decrease of 5.7 per cent.

8. The International Merchandise Trade Statistics report for June 2026 can be accessed through the
   DEPS’s website at www.deps.gov.bn.

 The first wave of fuel shortages in Russia began in May 2026, and had spread to all regions of Russia by July 2026. [ 12 ] 

 In the middle of 2026, Ukrainian missile and drone strikes on key Russian energy infrastructure resulted in an expansion of the crisis, with Politico saying that two-thirds of Russia's regions were reporting fuel supply issues, affecting millions of Russians as well as threatening businesses. In Crimea, a state of emergency was declared, with authorities banning all fuel sales. The Moscow Times said that the fuel shortage also resulted in a disruption of Crimea's tourism industry.

[DEPA, Greece] https://www.gasprocessingnews.com/news/2024/03/greeces-depa-files-for-arbitration-over-prices-in-gazprom-deal/
  "In January 2022, DEPA signed a contract with Gazprom for 2 billion cubic meters (bcm) of gas per year through 2026",
  price 80% TTF / 20% oil; take-or-pay with untaken volumes carried over. Earlier 2014 extension to 2026 provided for
  2.5-3 bcm/yr with more flexible take-or-pay (https://www.iene.eu/greeces-depa-gazprom-agree-long-term-gas-deal-p6494.html).

[Orsted, Denmark] https://www.euronews.com/next/2022/05/31/ukraine-crisis-russia-denmark-germany-gas
  Reuters via Euronews: the Orsted contract "stipulated gas supplies of up to 1.2 billion cubic metres per year";
  Gazprom Export halted it from 1 June 2022 after Orsted refused to pay in roubles. Shell Energy's German contract
  (up to 1.2 bcm/yr) was halted the same day.

[Serbia] https://seenews.com/news/serbia-hopes-to-sign-long-term-gas-delivery-contract-with-gazprom-by-may-15-777239
  "The previous long-term natural gas delivery agreement of Serbia with Gazprom expired on December 31, 2021. In
  November, Gazprom agreed to continue selling Serbia gas at the price of $270 ... until June 2022." Gazprom's
  exports to Serbia: 1.346 bcm in 2020; first 11 months of 2021 were 57% higher.

[SPP, Slovakia] https://spectator.sme.sk/business/c/slovakia-extends-russian-gas-contract-by-20-years (25 Dec 2008)
  20-year agreement effective 1 January 2009, about 130 bcm by 2028; Eustream transport contract to the same date.
  Interfax (18 Jun 2025, extract interfax_2025-06_spp_2028.txt): SPP says stopping Russian imports from 1 Jan 2028
  could expose it to take-or-pay claims of about EUR 16bn. Bruegel 2024 (extract): SPP contract end 2028, ACQ 67 TWh.

[Gasum, Finland] https://tass.com/economy/1621011 (extract tass_2023-05_gasum_termination.txt)
  Pipeline contract to end-2031 (TASS); annual volume not published. Stockholm award (Nov 2022) found the rouble
  decree force majeure and ordered Gasum to pay over EUR 300m incl. take-or-pay; Gasum terminated 22 May 2023.

[Eni, Italy] https://eegas.com/media/061115-EGM.htm and Energy Intelligence (2006)
  2006 strategic agreement extended Eni's Gazprom contracts to 2035 (another 2006 report: to 2027). Eni's contracts
  were "24 Bcm/yr" growing to 26; Eni ceded 2-3 bcm/yr for Gazprom's direct sales in Italy. Elcano (2024,
  https://media.realinstitutoelcano.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/policy-paper-the-future-of-russian-gas-in-the-eu.pdf):
  Eni "estimated to hold contracts for more than 20 bcm a year"; OMV 7 bcm/yr to 2040 (2018 contract).

[Uniper, Germany] https://www.uniper.energy/news/uniper-terminates-russian-gas-supply-contracts
  Award of 7 June 2024 (Stockholm, Swiss law): right to terminate and over EUR 13bn damages for gas not delivered
  since mid-2022; contracts ran to the mid-2030s; no volumes since end-August 2022. Annual volume not in the results.

[Engie, France] https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/?p=914724 (extract hellenicshipping_2022-03_engie.txt)
  Long-term Gazprom contracts = about 20% of Engie's 2021 gas sales; France bought 12.4 bcm from Gazprom in 2020.
  Contracts extended to 2030 (Gazprom, Dec 2008; fuel_volume_cf.json contracts).

[Latvia] https://news.err.ee/1608671326/gazprom-announces-suspension-of-supplies-to-latvia
  Gazprom halted supplies to Latvia (30 Jul 2022) "for violating conditions"; Latvia's law bans Russian gas from
  1 Jan 2023. Latvijas Gaze kept buying via an intermediary. Contract end date not found (Cedigaz 2018 shows the
  Latvian contract ending in 2017; renewal terms not public).

[Eesti Gaas, Estonia] https://www.baltictimes.com/news/articles/8663/
  Eesti Gaas extended its Gazprom contract to 2015 (2003 report). Gazprom sold its stake in 2016. No long-term
  contract after 2015 found; 2019-2021 purchases were short-term (site volumes: 0.48, 0.21, 0.06 bcm).

[Turkiye] https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/1804813 (extract
  argus_2021_turkey_contracts.txt): Botas 20 bcm/yr (16 Blue Stream + 4 Western Line); seven private firms 10 bcm/yr;
  take-or-pay 80%. Argus (2021): "14bn m3/yr through Turkish Stream, 8bn m3/yr of which expires this year".
  S&P Global (11 Oct 2024): Blue Stream 16 bcm/yr expires end-2025; Enerco 2.5 bcm/yr to end-2025; Avrasya 0.5 to
  Feb 2026. TASS (Dec 2025, extract): Botas renewed 21.75 bcm/yr for one year.

[Austria after 16 Nov 2024] https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2024-12-02/austria-end-direct-gas-supplies-gazprom
  and https://bnnbloomberg.ca/investing/2024/11/16/russian-gas-flows-are-steady-even-after-gazprom-cut-off-omv
  OSW: Russian gas kept flowing through Ukraine at similar volumes and "is supplied to the Austrian market in slightly
  smaller volumes and no longer directly by Gazprom", most likely spot sales at Baumgarten. Another report: Baumgarten
  flows fell 10-20% after the OMV halt (OMV had taken about 17 mcm/d).

[IEA, March 2022] https://www.iea.org/reports/a-10-point-plan-to-reduce-the-european-unions-reliance-on-russian-natural-gas
  Gazprom contracts covering more than 15 bcm/yr expire by end-2022 (about 12% of Gazprom's 2021 EU supplies); close
  to 40 bcm/yr by the end of the decade.

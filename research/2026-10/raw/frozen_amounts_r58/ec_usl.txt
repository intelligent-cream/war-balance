The loan will be implemented under enhanced cooperation, in line with the Council Decision authorising its establishment. The total envelope is indicatively divided into €30 billion for budgetary assistance and €60 billion for defence procurement support. This reflects a balanced approach addressing both Ukraine’s immediate macroeconomic needs and its urgent security requirements.

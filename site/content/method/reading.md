# How to read a number

Germany, 2022 to August 2026, in the current build:

1. **Supported Ukraine with 1.79%<!-- n:DEU.ua -->** of a year's GDP (EUR 74.7bn<!-- n:DEU.uaEur -->): it gave EUR 76.1bn<!-- n:DEU.uaGave --> and earned back 1.39<!-- n:DEU.uaBackEur --> from the war effort.
2. **Cut Russia's earnings from trade by 1.74%<!-- n:DEU.rus -->** (EUR 72.6bn<!-- n:DEU.rusEur -->): Russia kept that much less from Germany than without the war. EUR 22.0bn<!-- n:DEU.rusCut --> of it is gas Russia cut off itself, which the panel labels.
3. **Net: toward Ukraine by 3.65%<!-- n:DEU.net -->**, likely 2.58%<!-- n:DEU.netLo --> to 5.05%<!-- n:DEU.netHi -->. The net adds help given and a cut in Russia's earnings, two different things, plus Russian money frozen (EUR 5.3bn<!-- n:DEU.frozenEur -->).
4. **A total, not a yearly rate**: over about 4.7 years, so an average year is about 0.7%.

What to look at:

- **"Compared with what?"** The wide range comes almost entirely from "bought less from Russia": nobody knows exactly what Germany would have bought without the war ([counterfactual](#view=how&sec=m-counterfactual)).
- **Aid differs from Kiel's.** Kiel's figure for Germany is EUR 49.5bn; 25.9 is counted because donated weapons are at market value, loans at their gift value, and military aid counts when delivered.
- **The likely range** is about 90%, set by judgement. The **method band** (net 3.13%<!-- n:DEU.netBandLo --> to 4.05%<!-- n:DEU.netBandHi -->) shows the same number under other reasonable method choices ([ranges](#view=how&sec=m-ranges)).
- **The letter C** means most of the number rests on modelled parts ([certainty letters](#view=how&sec=m-ranges-certainty-letters)). A = reported, B = measured estimate, C = modelled, D = rough.
- **A `*`** on a part: its 2026 value is estimated from the 2025 rate.
- **2026 prices**: 2022–2025 alone the net is +2.94%<!-- n:DEU.net2025 -->; 2026 adds a lot because fuel prices rose with the Hormuz crisis ([prices](#view=how&sec=m-prices-2026-and-the-hormuz-crisis)).
- **Not in the number**: what Russia took from German companies (seizures EUR 9.5bn, forced exits 2.4bn) is a marker on the map.
- **Each part opens its method**: tap a part in the panel, then "How it works" for that money flow, its sources and its changes.
